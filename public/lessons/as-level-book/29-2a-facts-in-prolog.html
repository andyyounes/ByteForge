29.03 Facts in Prolog
A clause without a body — stating something is simply true.
Theory

What a fact is

  • A clause WITHOUT a body is a FACT.
capitalCity(paris). capitalCity(berlin). capitalCity(cairo).
  • Clause 1 means: "Paris is a capital city." capitalCity(paris) is a COMPOUND TERM; both capitalCity and paris are ATOMS.
  • capitalCity is the PREDICATE; paris is its ARGUMENT. Since it takes ONE argument, its arity is 1 — written capitalCity/1.

Running a query against facts

?- capitalCity(paris). true. ?- capitalCity(london). false.
  • "true" means: YES, Paris is a capital city (matches a fact in the knowledge base exactly).
  • "false" means: NO — but ONLY because London simply isn't INCLUDED as a fact. Prolog has NO real-world knowledge; it only knows what's explicitly stated.

Practical checklist if a query gets no response

  • Has the knowledge base actually been CONSULTED (loaded) first?
  • Are lower-case letters used correctly for atoms?
  • Does the query end with a FULL STOP?
Practice — Test Yourself

These questions only cover what THIS subsection taught — facts, atoms, predicates, and arity (completes Section 29.03).

Short answer 1
What is a fact in Prolog?
Reveal answer

A clause without a body — a simple statement asserting something is true.

Short answer 2
Why does capitalCity(london). return false, if London genuinely is a capital city in the real world?
Reveal answer

Prolog has no real-world knowledge — it only knows what is explicitly stated in the knowledge base. Since the fact capitalCity(london). was never added, Prolog correctly reports it cannot confirm the statement.

Fill in the blank 3
Every clause in Prolog must end with a ______.
Reveal answer

Full stop.

Spot the error 4
A student writes the fact capitalCity(Paris). with a capital P, expecting to state that Paris is a capital city. What actually happens?
Reveal answer

Since Paris starts with a capital letter, Prolog treats it as a VARIABLE, not the atom representing the city — this fact wouldn't mean what the student intended at all.

Applied / scenario 5
Write the arity notation for a predicate parent(fred, jack).
Reveal answer

parent/2 (it takes two arguments).

Short answer 6
Before running a query, what must be done to the knowledge base file first?
Reveal answer

It must be consulted (loaded) into the Prolog environment.
