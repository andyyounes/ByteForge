2.01 Cable Transmission Media
Twisted pair, coaxial, fibre-optic — the same job, three genuinely different trade-offs.
Key Terms
Bandwidth
The data transmission rate a medium can achieve.
Attenuation
Deterioration of a signal, worse at higher frequencies.
Repeater
A device that boosts a weakening signal along a transmission line.
Theory

Four factors that decide which cable to use

  • Cost — of the cable itself and its connecting devices.
  • Bandwidth — governs the maximum possible data rate.
  • Interference — how easily transmitted signals are corrupted by external sources.
  • Attenuation — signal deterioration at high frequencies, which increases the need for repeaters/amplifiers.

Reading the table — fibre-optic wins on performance, but not on cost

  • Fibre-optic performs BEST on every technical measure — but costs the most.
  • For a genuinely NEW installation, fibre-optic's performance advantage usually wins out.
  • Where copper cable is ALREADY installed, the cost of ripping it out and replacing it with fibre may not be justified — a real, practical trade-off, not just "always pick the best spec".

Where each cable type is actually used today

  • Twisted pair: still near-universal for connecting a telephone handset to the line, and the technology of choice for high-speed LANs.
  • Coaxial: mostly replaced for long-distance telephone cabling, but still extensively used by cable TV companies and often in metropolitan area networks (MANs).
  • Fibre-optic: the technology of choice for long-distance cabling. A fibre-optic cable bundles many individual fibres together; coaxial cable is NOT bundled this way.
Practice — Test Yourself

These questions only cover what THIS subsection taught — twisted pair, coaxial, and fibre-optic cable.

Short answer 1
Name the four factors used to compare cable types.
Reveal answer

Cost, bandwidth (data rate), interference, and attenuation (which affects how often repeaters are needed).

Short answer 2
Which cable type performs best on bandwidth, attenuation, AND interference, all at once?
Reveal answer

Fibre-optic — highest bandwidth, least attenuation, least interference.

Fill in the blank 3
A fibre-optic cable bundles many individual ______, whereas coaxial cable is ______.
Reveal answer

Fibres; not bundled.

Spot the error 4
A student concludes that every organisation should always replace its existing copper cabling with fibre-optic immediately, since fibre performs best on every metric. Why is this an oversimplification?
Reveal answer

Fibre-optic also costs the MOST — where copper cable is already installed and working, the cost of replacing it may not be justified by the performance gain. The best choice for a NEW installation isn't automatically the best choice for an existing one.

Applied / scenario 5
A company is laying brand-new cabling for a long-distance link between two cities. Which cable type would generally be chosen, and why?
Reveal answer

Fibre-optic — it's the technology of choice for long-distance cabling, with the best bandwidth and least attenuation/interference, and for a new installation the extra cost is usually justified by this performance.

Short answer 6
Which cable type is still near-universal for connecting a telephone handset to its line?
Reveal answer

Twisted pair.
