1.2 Computers in Life & IT Careers
Where computers show up in daily life, the technologies shaping tomorrow, and seven real IT careers.
Theory — Computers in Different Fields of Life

Everywhere, every day

  • Computers are used across manufacturing, e-commerce, education, medicine, banking, communication, entertainment, engineering, agriculture, architecture, business, defense, and sports.
  • News, weather updates, travel bookings, money transfers, and taxi bookings are all now handled through mobile apps.
  • School, library, and hospital management are examples of managerial tasks now automated by software.

Emerging technologies shaping tomorrow

  • Artificial Intelligence (AI) — machines simulating human intelligence: learning, reasoning, problem-solving. Used in scientific research, healthcare, and space technology. Machine Learning is a part of AI — it learns from routines to suggest options (e.g. a maps app suggesting your commute).
  • Robotics — robots are machines controlled by a computer (or external device) that carry out complex actions automatically. Common in manufacturing, industry, science, medicine, and education.
  • Wireless Communication — transmits information via electromagnetic waves (Infrared, Radio Frequency, satellite). GPS finds real-time locations; 3G/4G/5G transformed phones into fast mobile-internet devices.
  • Virtual Reality (VR) — an artificial environment created with software that feels real to the user; used for training, education, games, and interactive stories.
Theory — Seven IT Careers
Software Engineer
Uses programming languages to build software products — games, business apps, educational/entertainment software.
Network Administrator
Installs, maintains, and upgrades the hardware/software needed to run an organization's network.
Database Administrator (DBA)
Maintains a secure database environment for an office, business, or organization.
Web Designer
Builds websites — content, design, images, text — and maintains/updates them afterward.
Graphic Designer
Develops layouts for brochures, magazines, and adverts using computer graphics software.
Information Security Analyst
Plans and carries out security measures to prevent data/information loss — "the police force of the internet".
Computer Science / IT Teacher
Teaches Computer Science in schools, colleges, and universities.
Practice — Test Yourself

These questions only cover what this subsection taught — computers in daily life, emerging technologies, and IT careers.

Short answer 1
What is the difference between Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning, as described here?
Reveal answer

AI is the broader idea of machines simulating human intelligence (learning, reasoning, problem-solving). Machine Learning is a part of AI that specifically learns from routines/data to suggest options.

Short answer 2
Which career is responsible for maintaining a secure database environment?
Reveal answer

A Database Administrator (DBA).

Fill in the blank 3
Wireless communication transmits information using electromagnetic waves such as ______ and Radio Frequency.
Reveal answer

Infrared (IR).

Spot the error 4
A student says an Information Security Analyst's job is to design website layouts. What's wrong?
Reveal answer

That's a Graphic Designer's or Web Designer's job. An Information Security Analyst plans and carries out security measures to prevent data/information loss.

Applied / scenario 5
A company wants to train staff using a realistic simulated environment without real-world risk. Which technology fits best?
Reveal answer

Virtual Reality (VR) — it creates an artificial environment that feels real, specifically useful for training and education.

Short answer 6
Name one career responsible for building and maintaining a website's content and design.
Reveal answer

Web Designer.
