4.3 Communication Devices
Switch, router, three kinds of modem, and the Network Interface Card.
Key Terms
Switch
Connects computers/devices on a network via plugged-in data cables.
Router
Connects two or more networks; finds the optimal data path (network traffic controller).
Modem
Modulator/Demodulator — converts between digital and analog signals.
NIC
Network Interface Card — hardware connecting a computer physically to a network.

Switch vs. Router

  • Switch — connects computers and devices (printers, scanners, cameras) on ONE network via data cables.
  • Router — connects TWO OR MORE networks together; a combination of hardware and software, its main job is determining the optimal data path (it's a "network traffic controller").

Modem — Modulation & Demodulation

  • Modulation — converting DIGITAL signals into ANALOG signals.
  • Demodulation — the opposite: converting ANALOG signals into DIGITAL signals.
  • Dial-up Modem — uses standard telephone lines (which only carry analog), converting the computer's digital data to analog to send it.
  • DSL Modem — also uses telephone lines, but has a built-in network switch and delivers data/voice at higher speed than dial-up; some have wireless too.
  • ISDN Modem — transmits data, voice, AND video over a normal phone line at the same time; faster and more expensive; converts analog voice to digital before transmission.

Network Interface Card (NIC)

  • A hardware device installed on the motherboard that establishes the physical connection between a computer and the network.
  • Translates computer data into electrical signals sent to the network.
  • Modern motherboards have built-in NICs.
Practice — Test Yourself

These questions only cover what this subsection taught — switches, routers, modems, and NICs.

Short answer 1
What is the key difference between a switch and a router?
Reveal answer

A switch connects devices WITHIN one network. A router connects TWO OR MORE networks together and determines the optimal data path between them.

Short answer 2
What does "modulation" mean in the context of a modem?
Reveal answer

Converting a digital signal into an analog signal — needed because telephone lines only carry analog signals, while computer data is digital.

Fill in the blank 3
Among the three modem types, the ______ modem can transmit data, voice, and video at the same time over a normal telephone line.
Reveal answer

ISDN.

Spot the error 4
A student says the NIC's job is to determine the best path for data across multiple networks. What's wrong?
Reveal answer

That's the router's job. The NIC's job is to physically connect a single computer to the network and translate its data into electrical signals.

Applied / scenario 5
A home user wants faster internet than dial-up but doesn't want to install a brand new switch separately. Which modem type fits, and why?
Reveal answer

A DSL modem — it has a BUILT-IN network switch and delivers data/voice at higher speed than dial-up, over the same telephone lines.

Short answer 6
Where is a NIC typically installed in a computer?
Reveal answer

On the motherboard (modern motherboards often have one built in).
