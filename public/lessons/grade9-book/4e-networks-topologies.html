4.4-4.5 Computer Networks & Topologies
LAN/MAN/WAN by size, and bus/ring/star by physical layout.
Theory — Network & Networking
  • Computer Network — a group of computers and related equipment (printers, scanners, servers) connected to share data and resources.
  • Networking — the act of joining computers/accessories together so information exchange and resource sharing can happen.
Theory — Three Network Sizes

LAN

Local Area Network. Limited area — school, lab, home, office. Built with inexpensive hardware (hubs, switches, cables).

MAN

Metropolitan Area Network. A whole city/campus/region. Fast but expensive — usually large orgs/universities.

WAN

Wide Area Network. Cities, provinces, countries. Uses satellites/microwaves; may include multiple MANs/LANs; expensive, dedicated connections.

Theory — Three Network Topologies (Physical Layout)

Bus Topology

  • All devices connect to a single central cable (the "backbone").
  • Advantages: simple, low cost, easy to expand.
  • Disadvantage: if the bus cable breaks, the ENTIRE network goes down.

Ring Topology

  • Devices connected in a circle; the signal travels around the loop in one direction, passing through each computer (each acting as a repeater).
  • Disadvantage: failure of one link or computer can make the ENTIRE network non-functional.

Star Topology

  • All computers connect to a central hub/switch; to communicate, a sender always goes through the hub first.
  • Advantages: easy to set up and expand; if ONE link to the hub breaks, only that one station is affected — not the whole network.
Practice — Test Yourself

These questions only cover what this subsection taught — LAN/MAN/WAN and the three topologies.

Short answer 1
What's the key advantage of Star Topology over Bus Topology when a single cable/link fails?
Reveal answer

In Star Topology, if one link to the hub breaks, only that one station is affected. In Bus Topology, a break in the single shared cable brings down the ENTIRE network.

Short answer 2
A university wants to connect all its buildings across one city campus. Which network size fits best?
Reveal answer

MAN (Metropolitan Area Network) — it's designed for coverage across a city or campus.

Fill in the blank 3
In Ring Topology, if one link or computer fails, the ______ network can become non-functional.
Reveal answer

entire.

Spot the error 4
A student says LAN is used to connect computers across different countries. What's wrong?
Reveal answer

That's WAN's job. LAN covers a limited local area like a school, home, or office building.

Applied / scenario 5
A small office wants a network that's cheap, simple to set up, and easy to expand later. Which topology fits best?
Reveal answer

Bus Topology — simplicity, low cost, and easy expansion are its exact advantages (though it comes with the risk of total failure if the main cable breaks).

Short answer 6
In Star Topology, how does a sender communicate with another computer on the network?
Reveal answer

It must send the information to the central hub/switch first, which then transmits it to the destination computer.
