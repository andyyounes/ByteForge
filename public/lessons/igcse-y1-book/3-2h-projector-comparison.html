3.2h DLP vs. LCD Projectors — Advantages & Disadvantages
Two competing projector technologies, each with genuine, real trade-offs worth knowing exactly.
Theory

Why the trade-offs matter

DLP and LCD projectors both display computer output onto a large screen, but their different internal mechanisms (millions of tiny mirrors vs. three coloured LCD panels) lead to genuinely different real-world strengths and weaknesses — useful when choosing the right projector for a specific application.

DLP — Advantages
  • Higher contrast ratios
  • Higher reliability/longevity
  • Quieter running than LCD
  • Single DMD chip — no issues lining up images
  • Smaller and lighter than LCD
  • Better suited to dusty/smoky atmospheres
DLP — Disadvantages
  • Can suffer "shadows" during moving images
  • No true grey components in the image
  • Colour definition/saturation often worse than LCD
LCD — Advantages
  • Sharper image than DLP
  • Better colour saturation than DLP
  • More energy-efficient than DLP — generates less heat
LCD — Disadvantages
  • Contrast ratios not as good as DLP (though improving)
  • Limited lifespan — less longevity than DLP
  • LCD panels are organic — degrade over time (screens yellow, colours degrade)
Practice — Test Yourself

These questions only cover what THIS subsection taught — DLP vs. LCD projector advantages and disadvantages.

Short answer 1
Which projector type is generally quieter and smaller — DLP or LCD?
Reveal answer

DLP — it's quieter running and smaller/lighter than LCD.

Short answer 2
Why does an LCD projector generate less heat than a DLP projector?
Reveal answer

LCD projectors are more energy-EFFICIENT than DLP technology, and as a direct consequence, generate less heat.

Fill in the blank 3
LCD panels are ______ in nature, which means they tend to ______ over time — the screen turns yellow and colours degrade.
Reveal answer

Blank 1: organic. Blank 2: degrade.

Spot the error 4
A student claims: "DLP projectors are strictly better than LCD projectors in every way, since they're the newer technology." What real counter-evidence from this lesson contradicts this?
Reveal answer

LCD projectors genuinely beat DLP in several specific areas: sharper images, better colour saturation, and better energy efficiency (less heat generated). DLP has its own real advantages (contrast, reliability, size, noise, dust tolerance), but it is not strictly better in every respect.

Applied / scenario 5
A construction site needs a portable projector for daily briefings in a genuinely dusty environment, where reliability and low maintenance matter more than absolute colour accuracy. Which projector type should be chosen, and why, based on this lesson's content?
Reveal answer

DLP. The lesson states DLP projectors are better suited to dusty or smoky atmospheres, and offer higher reliability/longevity — both a direct match for this scenario's priorities, even though LCD would offer better colour saturation (a lower priority here).

Short answer 6
Name one genuine drawback of DLP projectors when displaying moving images.
Reveal answer

The image can suffer from "shadows" when showing a moving image.
