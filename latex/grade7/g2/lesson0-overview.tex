\begin{frame}{Real-World Example}
    \Large
    Any RPG or shooter's health bar visibly shrinking as you take damage — the bar's pixel width is driven directly by one number, exactly like this chapter's health-bar challenge. Minecraft's ten hearts are the same idea drawn as icons instead of a bar — hearts remove as the health variable drops.
\end{frame}


\begin{frame}{Let Them Discover}
    \Large
    Find a game with a visible health or loading bar. Watch it shrink or fill — does it move in smooth small steps, or big jumps? What does that tell you about how often the variable is being redrawn?
\end{frame}
